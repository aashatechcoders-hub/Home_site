1. Commitment to Privacy & Confidentiality

ATC (Aasha Tech Coders) is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and data integrity of our clients, website visitors, and partner organizations. We follow a strict principle of minimal data collection: we only collect information necessary to deliver exceptional software engineering, AI agent architectures, and cloud services.

2. Client Code, Data & 100% IP Ownership Guarantee

We operate under a strict 100% Client IP Ownership model. Any software, AI models, custom codebases, configurations, and architecture designs developed by ATC under client agreements belong entirely to the client upon milestone settlement. ATC does not claim ownership, sell, or license your proprietary codebase to third parties.

3. Security & Compliance Standards

Security is built into our foundational engineering cycle, not bolted on after launch. Our technical practices align with modern cloud and data protection frameworks:

  • Encryption in Transit: All HTTP traffic is enforced over TLS 1.3 with automated HSTS headers.
  • Encryption at Rest: Cloud databases, persistent volumes, and S3 vaults utilize AES-256 standard encryption.
  • Tenant Isolation: For multi-tenant SaaS products, logical and database-level isolation guarantees strict data separation.
  • Zero Credential Leakage: Secrets, API keys, and connection strings are managed via KMS/environment vaults, never committed into code repositories.
  • Automated Vulnerability Scanning: CI/CD pipelines run automated dependency audits and SAST checks before production deployment.
