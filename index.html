Frequently Asked Questions

QUESTIONS FOUNDERS ASK.

The things most clients want to know before their first call with us.

How do we start working together? +

Fill out the project inquiry form below or message us on WhatsApp. We schedule a free 30-minute discovery call to understand your goals, current stack, and timeline, then send a written scope and quote — no obligation before that.

Do you work with early-stage startups, or only established companies? +

Both. We regularly work with solo founders validating a first MVP as well as growing businesses that need a dedicated engineering partner. Engagement models are structured differently for each — see our Engagement & Pricing section above.

Who owns the code, infrastructure, and IP after launch? +

You do — fully. Every project ships with complete source code, documentation, and infrastructure access under your accounts. We don't hold your product hostage on our own cloud accounts or private repositories.

What's a realistic timeline for a project? +

It depends entirely on scope. A focused MVP or landing product typically takes a few weeks; multi-module platforms, AI agent integrations, or enterprise systems take longer. We give you a milestone-based timeline in writing before work starts, not a vague estimate.

Can you work with our existing team or codebase? +

Yes. We regularly join as an embedded engineering pod alongside in-house teams, or take over an existing codebase for continued development, refactoring, or a cloud/DevOps overhaul.

Do you sign NDAs and handle sensitive data? +

Yes, we sign NDAs before any detailed technical discussion when requested, and can operate under formal data-handling and security agreements for Enterprise engagements.

What happens after launch — do you disappear? +

No. Every Project Sprint includes a 30-day bug-fix window, and most clients move into a lightweight Growth Partner retainer afterward for monitoring, patches, and continued feature work. Support is month-to-month with no long-term lock-in.

How do we communicate during the project? +

Directly with the engineers building your product — via WhatsApp, email, or Slack, plus scheduled milestone check-ins. No account managers relaying messages between you and the people writing the code.
